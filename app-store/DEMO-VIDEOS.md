# BuzzBee — demo video plan

Six short recordings, each proving one feature actually works end-to-end on
a real device. One brief sentence per video summarizes what it's demonstrating;
the steps under it are the shot list.

---

## 1st video — Fixed-time alarm rings and dismisses, app open or fully quit

Proves a fixed-time alarm reliably rings and can be dismissed both while
BuzzBee stays open and after a full force-quit.

- Create fixed time alarm
- Stay still in home app
- Trigger Alarm
- Dismiss Alarm
- Create fixed time alarm
- Quit the app
- Trigger Alarm
- Dismiss Alarm

## 2nd video — Record Your Own Alarm Sound works fully offline

Proves recording, previewing, and deleting a custom alarm sound needs no
network connection at all.

- Turn off wifi
- Create a custom sound
- Play the sound
- Delete the sound

## 3rd video — Calendar Auto-Shift nudges the wake window around a conflict

Proves BuzzBee notices a conflicting event on tomorrow's calendar and offers
to shift the alarm's wake window.

- Create Alarm
- Quit the app
- Create meeting event
- Open App
- Push notification to move the alarm

## 4th video — Bedtime Reminder opens the Wind-Down screen

Proves the Bedtime Reminder notification, tapped from outside the app,
correctly launches the Wind-Down breathing screen through to completion.

- Set a bedtime reminder
- Set bed time
- Set bedtime reminder
- Quit App
- Push a notification
- Click the notification
- Open the wind-down mode screen
- Click Done

## 5th video — Wake Window escalates to hard deadline, dismissed via Clap

Proves a Wake Window alarm rings through to its hard deadline and can still
be dismissed by completing the Clap mission after tapping the native alert.

- Create a wake window alarm
- Quit App
- Trigger alarm
- Wait until hard deadline
- Click the alarm kit notification
- Finish the Clap mission

## 6th video — Reset Data returns the app to a fresh-install state

Proves Reset Data fully wipes every alarm and setting, with nothing left
behind after quitting and reopening the app.

- Open App
- Has existing data
- Reset everything
- Quit App
- Open App — no data
