Frequently asked questions

Why didn't my alarm go off?

This is almost always about the app's state when the deadline arrived. BuzzBee works most reliably while it's open (foreground or backgrounded, not force-quit) — it keeps a quiet background session alive specifically so your alarm can still fire while you're not actively looking at the app. If BuzzBee is fully force-quit (swiped away in your app switcher), no third-party app can run its own code on iOS at all, so it falls back to a backup system notification instead, which uses a shorter, non-looping sound. If your alarm didn't ring, check: was the app force-quit, is Do Not Disturb / a Focus mode blocking alerts, and is your media volume up? We recommend testing BuzzBee a few times with your own device and settings before relying on it for anything time-critical.

What is Smart Wake, and how does it decide when to ring?

Instead of one fixed time, you set a wake window (e.g. 6:30–7:00 AM) and a hard deadline. While that window is open, BuzzBee watches your phone's motion sensor for signs of lighter sleep — small, frequent movements rather than long stillness — and rings the moment it notices them. If nothing is detected, it rings at your hard deadline regardless, so it never fails to wake you — it only ever wakes you earlier and more gently when it can.

Does BuzzBee record my voice or conversations?

No. The microphone is used for Ambient Awareness, the Clap mission, and the Buzzzzz mission, but in every case BuzzBee only reads a live volume/loudness level — it never listens to, interprets, stores, or transmits actual audio content. See our Privacy Policy for the full details.

How do I change my alarm's sound or dismiss mission?

Open the alarm from the Home screen, then tap Choose a Mission or Sound — each opens a picker where you can preview and select a new option. Changes apply the moment you save the alarm.

Can I snooze, or dismiss without finishing the mission?

No — this is intentional. Snoozing and shortcut-dismissing are disabled by design, since the entire point of a mission is confirming you're actually awake. If a specific mission consistently doesn't work well for you (for example, Clap or Buzzzzz in a noisy room), try switching that alarm to Math or Tap instead, which don't rely on the microphone at all.

How do I turn off Wind-Down Mode, Calendar Auto-Shift, or Ambient Awareness?

These are global toggles in Settings → Smart Features — turning one off stops that feature (and its notifications, where applicable) for every alarm.

Is BuzzBee free?

Yes. BuzzBee currently works fully offline with no account, no sign-up, and no paywall.
